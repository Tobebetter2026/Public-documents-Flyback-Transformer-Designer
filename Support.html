Flyback Transformer Designer

Last updated: 2026-05-21

Flyback Transformer Designer is an offline flyback transformer design application tailored for power engineers: starting from electrical specifications and design constraints, it automatically completes core selection, turn ratio and wire gauge optimization, loss and temperature rise estimation, and generates archivable PDF / JSON reports. All calculations run locally on your device; design data is never uploaded to the developer's servers.

Available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac via the App Store (Apple silicon Macs run the iOS-compatible version, as shown in the App Store). Includes 14 days of full-featured free trial; continue with annual subscription after trial expires.

Product Advantages

  • Purpose-built for flyback transformers: Supports DCM / BCM / QR / CCM modes, up to 3 secondary outputs, covering common flyback topologies and multi-output power supplies.
  • Joint iterative selection: Core × turn ratio × wire gauge (including Litz wire) scanned in one pass, compare multiple solutions, eliminate tedious manual recalculation.
  • Built-in component databases: Integrated core, bobbin, and material data (E / ETD / PQ / RM / EFD, etc.), filterable by manufacturer.
  • Air gap and inductance alignment: Air gap analytically calculated from Lm and Np with fringing effect correction; Lm, B_peak, and temperature rise verified simultaneously.
  • Extensible databases: "My Database" maintains custom materials, cores, and bobbins on your device, merged with built-in libraries for selection.
  • Privacy and offline-first: Core design functionality works offline; no third-party ads; independent app from "Inductor Designer Expert" with no shared accounts or data.

Key Features

  • Rectified DC input, multi-output specifications, and cross-regulation advisory.
  • AP method core pre-selection, iGSE / Steinmetz core loss, Dowell harmonic correction for copper loss.
  • Window allocation, insulated wire gauge, and thermal class constraints.
  • Interface: Simplified Chinese / English.

Workflow (Three Steps)

  1. Specifications & Constraints — Enter input voltage, multi-output requirements, switching frequency, operating mode, and constraints (Bmax, Jmax, Ku, etc.).
  2. Core & Winding Design — Review candidate solutions; select final design by loss, temperature rise, flux density, and window utilization.
  3. Summary & Report — Verify parameter tables and warnings, export PDF or JSON files.
