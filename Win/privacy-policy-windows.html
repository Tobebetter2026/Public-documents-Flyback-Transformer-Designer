Privacy Policy (Windows Edition)

Last updated: 2026-06-07 · Applicable to "Flyback Transformer Designer" for Windows / Microsoft Store

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter "Policy") applies to the Windows edition of the "Flyback Transformer Designer" application distributed via Microsoft Store (hereinafter "Application," "we," or "us"). This Policy explains how we handle information related to your device. Please read it before using.

1. Introduction

"Flyback Transformer Designer" is a local computation and design assistance tool for flyback transformers, supporting multiple secondary outputs and operating modes such as DCM / BCM / QR / CCM. The interface offers Simplified Chinese and English display; language preference is primarily determined by your device's system language settings and does not change the data handling principles described in this Policy. The application's built-in core and material data are stored on-device via the installation package, not obtained from uploading your device data. We follow the principle of privacy-first with computation and data primarily completed locally on your device: your design content and "My Database" are by default stored only on your device; unless otherwise stated below, we do not collect personally identifiable information for advertising or third-party sharing.

2. How We Collect and Use Information

2.1 Basic Principles
This application does not upload your design schemes, transformer specification parameters, or "My Database" contents to the developer's servers; we cannot remotely read such data from your device.

2.2 Information Processed on Your Device
Data you input or generate includes but is not limited to:

  • Electrical specifications and design constraints (input voltage, multiple outputs, switching frequency, operating mode, duty cycle, and flux density / current density limits, etc.)
  • Core selection, turn ratio, wire gauge, air gap, loss, and temperature rise iteration and summary results
  • Locally saved design projects and interface preferences
  • Custom ferrite materials, cores, and bobbins in "My Database"

PDF reports and JSON design files you generate on your device are created locally; if you share them via system sharing, email, or other applications, they are handled by your chosen recipient according to their terms.

The above information is used solely to present calculation results and export files on your device and is not uploaded to the developer's servers.

2.3 Analytics and Diagnostics (Anonymous)
We may learn of device model, system version, and aggregated usage information through standard mechanisms provided by Microsoft (such as Microsoft Store analytics and anonymous crash diagnostics), without including your design details. You can manage diagnostic data sharing in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Diagnostics & feedback to limit such reporting.

3. Data Storage

Design data is primarily stored in the application's local data folder on your device. Uninstalling the application or clearing its data will delete local content according to system behavior. This application does not use iCloud or any other cloud backup service to store your design data; if Windows includes the application's local data in a system-level backup you have configured (e.g., File History), that is governed by the relevant Microsoft service's own terms, and we cannot access such backups.

This application does not store a local trial start date and does not grant paid-feature access from a local timestamp. The Microsoft Store 7-day full-feature free trial included with the annual subscription product, trial eligibility, purchase records, and auto-renewal are handled by Microsoft (Microsoft Store / Partner Center). The application reads valid subscription or trial license status via the official Windows.Services.Store API when needed, and may cache subscription status and expiration time locally for interface display and basic state continuity while offline.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell design data. In accordance with application distribution, payment, and system service requirements, Microsoft (Microsoft Store, Partner Center, subscriptions, and purchase/license records) and network communication recipients you actively choose (such as hosting providers of privacy policy or technical support pages opened in your browser) handle minimally necessary information related to their respective connections according to their own terms.

5. Third Parties and Application Web Pages

If you open instructional, privacy policy, or technical support pages provided with the application in your browser, static content is loaded over HTTPS and does not contain embedded tracking scripts for personal tracking. External links such as GitHub Issues are accessed at your own discretion via your browser.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is not designed for children under 13 years of age; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Control

You can manage design schemes and "My Database" within the application; uninstalling the application deletes on-device data. The Microsoft Store 7-day free trial included with the annual subscription product, purchase records, and auto-renewal are handled by Microsoft (Microsoft Store) according to its terms; related account and subscription settings are managed by you in Windows Settings and the Microsoft Store app (Settings → Apps → Subscriptions or the Microsoft Store "Subscriptions" page).

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Policy and update the "Last updated" date on this page; material changes will be communicated through Microsoft Store release notes or appropriate in-application notices. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

10. Windows Platform Notes

This application is distributed via the Microsoft Store as a packaged Windows desktop application (MSIX), built on a WebView2-based local interface. Regardless of which Windows device you use, the data handling principles remain the same: design and calculation take place on your current device, and this product does not transmit your design file contents to the developer.

The application requires no location, camera, microphone, or other unnecessary permissions to use core functionality; core iteration and loss calculation can be completed offline; opening Microsoft Store subscription pages, verifying purchase/trial status, or browsing web help requires network connectivity.

On first launch, Windows may perform standard application integrity checks (such as Microsoft Defender SmartScreen) as part of the platform's official security mechanism, which may require network connectivity; thereafter, the application can run offline. Design projects are stored in the application's local data folder and are not shared with other applications.

The Microsoft Store 7-day free trial included with the annual subscription product and the annual subscription are completed via Microsoft (including Microsoft Store subscription and trial period rules, as shown in-application and in the Microsoft Store), and we do not use subscriptions for third-party advertising profiling.
